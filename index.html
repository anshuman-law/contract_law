    HOW THE COURSE WORKS

    Every doctrine becomes a playable legal problem

    Each module assigns the learner a role, presents plausible choices, reveals the practical consequence before explaining the Indian law, and then changes one material fact so the learner must reason again.

    1. Scenario
    2. Role
    3. Decision
    4. Consequence
    5. Indian-law reveal
    6. Changed fact
    7. Replay
    8. Debrief

    Scope: general principles of Indian contract law. Indemnity, guarantee, bailment, pledge and agency are not covered under this course.
