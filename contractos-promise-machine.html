CONCEPT 1 · THE PUZZLE

The word “promise” is not enough

Contract law asks what the speaker was doing - not merely which word was used.

HOSTEL · 7:00 A.M.

“I promise I will wake you before the examination.”

Social assurance No request for assent to a legal bargain
MARKET · 5:00 P.M.

“I will sell you this identified scooter for ₹28,000. Reply by six.”

Potential legal proposal Definite terms expressed to obtain assent

First question: Was willingness expressed with a view to obtaining the other person’s assent? That is the statutory idea of a proposal in section 2(a).

CONCEPT 2 · THE LEGAL CHAIN

Section 2 is a vocabulary machine

Each legal word describes a different stage. The stages should not be collapsed into one another.

  1. s 2(a)ProposalWillingness expressed to obtain assent
  2. s 2(b)AcceptanceThe recipient signifies assent
  3. s 2(b)PromiseThe accepted proposal
  4. s 2(e)AgreementPromises forming consideration for each other
  5. s 2(h)ContractAn agreement enforceable by law
s 2(d) · CONSIDERATION

The act, abstinence or promise given at the promisor’s desire supplies the legally relevant exchange.

Central distinction: acceptance creates a promise; promises supported by the relevant exchange form an agreement; enforceability turns the agreement into a contract.

CONCEPT 3 · THE ENFORCEABILITY GATE

Section 10 asks whether the agreement may enter contract law

Formation gets the transaction to “agreement”. Section 10 then checks whether law will recognise it as a contract.

AGREEMENT
SECTION 10
CONTRACT
  • Parties competent to contract
  • Consent freely given
  • Lawful consideration
  • Lawful object
  • Not expressly declared void

Important: section 10 also preserves other laws that require writing, witnesses or registration. The gate is therefore a legal checklist, not a magic word.

CONCEPT 4 · FOUR POSSIBLE DESTINATIONS

The timing and location of the defect determine the status

Do not use “void”, “voidable” and “becomes void” interchangeably.

Agreement → Contract · s 2(h)

Enforceable by law.

Agreement ↛ Void agreement · s 2(g)

Not enforceable by law.

Agreement ⇢ Voidable contract · s 2(i)

Enforceable at one party’s option, but not the other’s.

Valid contract first → × Becomes void · s 2(j)

Later ceases to be enforceable.

Diagnostic habit: ask what formed, where the legal problem appeared, who can rely on it and when enforceability was lost.

Need the legal vocabulary? Open the quick glossary
Proposal
Willingness to do or abstain from doing something, expressed with a view to obtaining the other person’s assent.
Acceptance
The person receiving the proposal signifies assent to it.
Promise
A proposal becomes a promise when it is accepted.
Consideration
The act, abstinence or promise given at the promisor’s desire in exchange for the promise.
Agreement
The legally relevant arrangement produced by promises and their exchange.
Contract
An agreement enforceable by law.
Void agreement
An agreement that is not enforceable by law.
Voidable contract
A contract enforceable at the option of one party but not the other.
