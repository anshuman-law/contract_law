Enter The Enforceability Gate

SYSTEM MODEL

A gate with two lanes. The consideration lane opens if s 2(d) is satisfied. The exception lane opens only if every element of a specific s 25 sub-clause is present, and each element is displayed as a physical latch that must be visibly engaged. Readable state: a queue of eight promises, and for each, which latches are engaged and which are missing.

makes the player a family office lawyer with eight promises to make enforceable before a succession event, a fixed budget, and a registration office that takes days. The physics is that s 25's exceptions are conjunctive: near relation and natural love and affection and writing and registration must all be present, and the game refuses to let a strong showing on one element compensate for a missing other, which is exactly how the section operates and exactly the intuition learners bring and lose on. The strategic layer is resource allocation under a deadline. Registration is the scarcest resource, so the player must decide which promises truly need the s 25(1) route and which can be converted into ordinary consideration-supported bargains by adding a small reciprocal obligation, which is cheaper and faster but changes the character of the transaction and may have tax and family consequences the game surfaces. The time-barred debt promise under s 25(3) needs only writing and signature, so it is cheap, but the player must first determine whether the debt is in fact barred, and the game includes one debt that is not barred, where the s 25(3) route is unnecessary and the promise is unsupported for a different reason. Round three introduces a challenge from a disappointed relative, played by Claude, who attacks the weakest latch on each promise. The failure mode that teaches most is the player who registers a promise between parties who are not in a near relation, spending the scarcest resource on a lane that was never open.

DOCTRINAL SPINE

ICA ss 10 and 25. An agreement made without consideration is void, subject to the exceptions in s 25, which cover a written and registered promise made on account of natural love and affection between parties standing in a near relation, a promise to compensate wholly or in part a person who has already voluntarily done something for the promisor or something the promisor was legally compellable to do, and a written and signed promise to pay a debt barred by the law of limitation. Section 25 also confirms that a completed gift is unaffected, and Explanation 2 confirms that inadequacy does not by itself make an agreement void.

STRATEGIC PAYOFF

The player learns that consideration is the default price of enforceability and that the exceptions are not softer alternatives but harder ones, trading substantive scrutiny for formal proof. That inversion is the module's whole point.

WIN AND LOSS LOGIC

Win by having at least six of eight promises enforceable at the succession event within budget. Lose instructively by satisfying three of four elements on several promises, which yields nothing at all.

REPLAY HOOK

Replay with registration unavailable, which forces every promise onto either the consideration lane or s 25(2) and (3), and reveals how narrow those are.
