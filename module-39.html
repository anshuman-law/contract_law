Enter The Compliance Maze

SYSTEM MODEL

A maze in which the destination is a commercial objective and the walls are statutory prohibitions. Several routes exist. Readable state per route: whether it touches an express prohibition, how many intermediate steps it uses, whether each step has an independent commercial rationale, and a Purpose Trace showing how visibly the route's structure points back to the prohibited outcome.

runs a structuring engagement across four rounds. The player advises a client who wants an outcome that a statute prohibits directly, and must design a structure. Each structural step costs money and time, and each may be given an independent commercial rationale at additional cost. The physics is the second limb of s 23: a structure fails not because it is complicated but because its object is to defeat the statute, and independent rationale for each step is the principal thing that breaks the Purpose Trace. Claude plays a regulator with an investigation budget, and its policy is to follow the Purpose Trace, opening the step with the weakest independent rationale first. This produces a genuine arms race: the player can add steps (which lengthens the trace and may look worse), buy rationale (expensive, and only credible if the step genuinely does something), or abandon the objective and pursue a lawful alternative that delivers eighty per cent of the value, which is available from round one and which most players ignore until they have burned their budget. That option is deliberately present, because recognising when structuring is not worth it is the professional skill the module is really teaching. Round three adds a whistleblower who supplies the regulator with the client's internal instruction describing the object of the structure in plain words, which is the single most common way such schemes fail and which no amount of external rationale repairs. Failure mode is an elegant structure destroyed by one internal email.

DOCTRINAL SPINE

ICA s 23, specifically the first two limbs: consideration or object forbidden by law, and consideration or object of such a nature that if permitted it would defeat the provisions of any law. The second limb reaches arrangements that comply with the letter of a statute while defeating its purpose, and it is the doctrinal basis on which structured avoidance schemes fail.

STRATEGIC PAYOFF

The player learns that the defeat-the-provisions limb targets purpose rather than form, and therefore that the record of purpose is the real battleground. This is the interpretive axis at its most consequential and it generalises to tax, exchange control and competition practice.

WIN AND LOSS LOGIC

Win by achieving the objective (or the lawful eighty per cent alternative) with the structure surviving the regulator's investigation. Lose instructively by building a structure whose internal record states its object.

REPLAY HOOK

Replay with the internal record already in the regulator's hands from turn one, which forces the player to build only structures that can be defended on their stated purpose.
