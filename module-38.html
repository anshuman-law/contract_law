Enter The Two-Part Scanner

SYSTEM MODEL

A scanner with two independent chambers. The consideration chamber examines what is being exchanged; the object chamber examines what the transaction is for. Readable state: for each chamber, which of the five s 23 categories (if any) is triggered, and a composite Enforceability output that fails if either chamber fails.

MODEL VAR // Knowledge Of PurposeMODEL VAR // Legal PositionMODEL VAR // ProofMODEL VAR // Leverage

puts the player in a compliance role at a chemicals and equipment distributor. Twelve orders arrive over four weeks, each with a stated purpose, a price structure and a customer profile. The player must approve, refuse or approve with conditions, under a revenue target that makes blanket refusal a losing strategy. The physics is that s 23 bites on the object, so knowledge of the customer's purpose is legally material, which creates the game's central and uncomfortable mechanic: investigating purpose can destroy a sale by giving the seller knowledge it would rather not have. That mechanic drives the strategy. The player can approve quickly on a clean face, which maximises revenue and leaves them exposed if the purpose surfaces later, or investigate, which protects them but slows the pipeline and occasionally reveals a purpose that forces refusal of an otherwise profitable order. Approving with conditions (end-use certificates, restrictions on resale) is a middle path that costs less than refusal, and the game rewards players who work out which orders it suits. Week three introduces the fraudulent-consideration limb, where the price structure itself is the problem: an order priced to disguise a payment, which is unlawful in the consideration chamber even though the goods and the purpose are entirely legitimate. Week four brings an audit that tests every approved order against both chambers. Failure mode is a compliance officer who scrutinises purposes rigorously and approves a set of transactions whose payment structures are themselves unlawful.

Opponent policy

The opposing side attacks the weakest proved element, then adapts to any repeated tactic.

Resolver notes

Two independent resolver functions over the same transaction object, each returning a triggered category or null. Enforceability is the logical AND. knowledgeOfPurpose must be a mutable flag set by investigation actions, and the object resolver must depend on it, so the player can feel the perverse incentive and discuss it in the debrief.

DOCTRINAL SPINE

ICA s 23. The consideration or object of an agreement is unlawful if it is forbidden by law, or is of such a nature that if permitted it would defeat the provisions of any law, or is fraudulent, or involves or implies injury to the person or property of another, or the court regards it as immoral or opposed to public policy. Every agreement of which the object or consideration is unlawful is void, and s 23 applies to the consideration and to the object separately.

STRATEGIC PAYOFF

The player learns that s 23 is two tests wearing one number, and that in commercial practice the object limb turns on what the seller knew, which makes the design of the intake process a legal decision.

WIN AND LOSS LOGIC

Win by hitting the revenue target with zero orders failing the audit in either chamber. Lose instructively by scanning only one chamber, or by adopting a see-no-evil approval policy that collapses when purpose evidence surfaces.

REPLAY HOOK

Replay as a customer trying to obtain restricted goods lawfully, which shows how legitimate buyers are burdened by the same architecture and why end-use documentation exists.
