The formation chain (ss 2, 10)

Proposal 2(a) → acceptance 2(b) → consideration 2(d) → agreement 2(e) → contract 2(h). A defect early cannot be cured later, so counsel strikes the earliest weak link.

The s 10 gate

An agreement is a contract only with free consent (s 14), competent parties (s 11) and lawful object (s 23). Fail capacity or object and it's void; flaw consent and it's voidable — valid until the wronged party avoids it (ss 19, 19A).

Void vs voidable vs valid

Void = no contract ever (a minor's agreement, Mohori Bibee; an unlawful object, s 23). Voidable = binding until rescinded by the party whose consent was not free. Getting this classification right is the whole strategic game.

The honest surprise (s 2(d))

Past consideration is valid in India — but only if the act was done "at the desire of the promisor." And a bare gift promise can still bind under s 25(1): writing + registration + natural love & affection.
